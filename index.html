    How our recruiting process works

    1. 1 Intake & role definition We learn your team, culture and must-have skills, then define the role and timeline together.
    2. 2 Sourcing & outreach We search our network, job boards and professional communities to reach active and passive candidates.
    3. 3 Screening & shortlist Candidates are interviewed and skills-checked, so you receive a short list of qualified people.
    4. 4 Offer, placement & follow-up We coordinate interviews and offers, then check in after the start date to make sure the fit is right.
