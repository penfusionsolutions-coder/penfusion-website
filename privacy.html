Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

PenFusion Solutions LLC ("PenFusion Solutions", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through penfusionsolutions.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us, such as when you fill in our contact form or email us: your name, business email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and any details you include in your message. If you send us a resume, we collect the information it contains.

Like most websites, our hosting and font providers may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the services you ask about.
  • For recruiting: to match candidates with suitable roles and, with your agreement, share your profile with prospective employers.
  • To keep records of our business communications.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run this website and handle messages: our contact form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, fonts are served by Google Fonts, and our email is provided by Hostinger. These providers process information only as needed to deliver their services.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiries and candidate information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, by emailing info@penfusionsolutions.com.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
